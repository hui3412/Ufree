From a picture

Read a schedule screenshot

Crop the picture as tightly as possible around the full timetable. Keep all grid borders, the times on the left, and the weekday headings at the top visible, including any weekend columns. Remove extra space outside the timetable, but don’t cut off any schedule content. Use a clear, upright picture and check the detected times before saving. Your picture stays on this device.

Review

Edit the weekly schedule

Select a busy block in the grid to edit its day, times or wording below. Use a day’s + button to add a block. Green gaps show breaks between busy times; empty time is free. Labels and course details are optional. Compare detected times with the picture before saving.

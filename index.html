Help & Tips

Have an idea, found a bug, or want to suggest an improvement? Send feedback to ufree_info@proton.me.

Add and edit schedules

Open Schedules to import an Omnivox screenshot or create a timetable manually. Picture recognition runs on your device. Review the detected names, days, times, rooms and classes before saving, and correct any uncertain results. The stored-people list starts collapsed; choose Show to edit a saved timetable or its semester. PDF and JSON imports are also available.

Find who is free

Current time follows the clock. Turn it off to choose a day and time, including weekends. All shows everyone; Free shows available people. Class Soon means someone has a short gap before their next class. Use the magnifying glass beside GROUP to search names and nicknames; the search field opens below the buttons. Search respects All/Free and Groups. Leaving the search field closes it and clears the filter; press the magnifying glass to reopen it. The clear button keeps the field open. Select a person for their next class and today's timetable, then use Full schedule to see the week.

People and individual actions

Select a person's name to reveal Full schedule, +, the red trash icon and Share. On a small screen, the page scrolls to these details. + adds them to an existing group. Trash deletes their schedule from this device after confirmation. Share exports only that person's schedule as a URL, QR code or text code.

Groups and shared availability

Turn on GROUP and use the group management icon beside search to create or edit groups. Choose Edit beside a group to rename it and remove members with the red trash icons. A person can belong to several groups. Hide or show a group's members, and open its … menu to move the group to the top or view weekly availability. Save group applies the changes; Cancel discards them. Removing a member preserves their schedule and other group memberships. Group preferences also let you control whether ungrouped people appear.

Share and import

The export icon opens quick sharing with Everyone selected. Expand People to share to choose fewer people. Export Code creates text you can copy; Export QR displays a scannable code; Copy Link creates a URL. Times, class names and rooms are included by default. Use … to include teachers, course codes, semesters and other saved details. Open a received link, scan a QR with your phone camera, or paste a text code in Schedules → Import. Review the people before confirming; nothing is saved automatically. Anyone with your export can read the selected schedule details.

Reorder, pin and undo

Drag a person to reorder the list on desktop, or hold and move their card on touch screens. With the person's name focused, Alt + Up or Down moves them using the keyboard. Groups remember their own order. Pin people in Settings to keep them above unpinned people. The back arrow undoes recent schedule and ordering changes during the current page session.

Settings and notifications

Open the cog for nicknames, pinned people, notification preferences and appearance. Choose a light or dark theme and an accent colour. Notifications need your browser's permission; keep UFree open for its time checks. Nicknames, groups and preferences stay on this device and are not included in schedule exports.

Keep a backup

Schedules are saved in this browser on this device, with no automatic sync between devices. Export a code or a JSON backup from Schedules before clearing website data, switching browsers or changing devices. Import the backup to restore your timetables. Undo does not survive closing or reloading the page.

Privacy Notice

No accounts required. Use the entire app without signing up or creating an account.

No app data collection. UFree has no analytics or tracking. It does not collect or send your personal data, schedules, pictures, usage metrics or identifiers to an application server. Recognition, availability checks and export processing run on your device.

Stored locally. Your schedules and settings stay in your device's browser storage, protected by the browser's site-storage isolation. UFree does not encrypt that storage itself. Clearing website data removes your saved information.

Hosting and sharing. The website host and asset providers receive normal network requests, which can include your IP address and browser information. Only exports you choose to share reveal schedule data to recipients. Sending feedback by email shares what you include in that message.

Add UFree to your iPhone Home Screen with Shortcuts
  1. Copy UFree's website address from your browser, leaving out any shared-code fragment after #.
  2. Open Apple's Shortcuts app and tap + to create a shortcut.
  3. Add the URL action and paste UFree's address.
  4. Add Open URLs below it, using the URL from the first action.
  5. Name the shortcut UFree. Open its Details, then tap Add to Home Screen.
  6. Choose the name and icon, then tap Add. Tap the new icon to open UFree in your browser.
